ROOFTOP ESCAPE

Seowon High School · Outbreak Night
Language
🧟NIGHTMAREDark halls · zombies · blood · jump scares · heartbeat
🤖DAYLIGHT DRILLBright & friendly · silly helper-bots · paint splats · zero scares
Two modes: 🧟 NIGHTMARE — dark, zombies, blood, jump scares. 🤖 DAYLIGHT DRILL — bright, friendly robots, paint splats, zero scares (great for kids or warm-ups). The host's choice applies to the whole co-op squad.
Goal: climb from the entrance hall to the rooftop (6 floors) and board the rescue helicopter / drone.
Stairwell gates are locked — find keycards, restore power, hack the CCTV terminal (Floor 2) and pry boarded doors with the crowbar. You will have to go back down.
Listen. The ring around your crosshair lights up when enemies walk or run nearby (PUBG-style).
Look. Bloodstains (or paint splats!) around a door mean something inside. Flashlight [F] — it eats battery; grab batteries everywhere.
Flares [G] pull enemies away. Medkits [H] heal. Crouch [C] to sneak. Sprint [Shift] is loud.
Co-op: host a room and share the code — up to 4 survivors. Downed friends can be revived [hold E].
Requires a keyboard + mouse. Click the game once to lock the mouse.
A fan-made zombie escape · Three.js + WebAudio · free to deploy
