## One-paragraph project summary (for the form)

Rooftop Escape — Outbreak is a 3D first-person zombie-survival escape game that runs entirely in the browser with no download and no backend. A study night at Seowon High goes wrong; you wake in the entrance hall and must climb six floors to a midnight helicopter extraction through a strict 24-step mission chain — scavenging a flashlight, batteries, a crowbar and an SMG, reading a 24-segment directional threat ring, prying boarded doors, restoring power, decoding the CCTV archive, and making a 7-second decision about another survivor that changes the ending. The finale holds a landing circle for 30 seconds against a 34-zombie siege and a boss. It ships in two modes (Nightmare and a kid-friendly Daylight Drill), four languages (English, 中文, Tiếng Việt, Bahasa Indonesia), and 2–4 player online co-op with voice chat. Built with Three.js and the Web Audio API; a headless simulation harness plays the full mission chain on every change and currently passes 64/64 (Nightmare), 66/66 (Daylight), 64/64 (Chinese) and 61/61 (co-op) checks, plus a two-browser co-op test at 17/17.
